"You should drop by sometime," the acquaintance had said at that university reception, the kind of throwaway invitation that most people recognize as mere social courtesy. Yet here Alex was, having taken it at face value with the earnestness of someone chronically uncertain about social cues.

The radio crackled with a Mexican ballad, all passionate vocals and intricate guitar work that seemed to pulse with a confidence Alex had never possessed. The Spanish lyrics washed over the car's interior, beautiful but impenetrable, representing everything about life that existed beyond Alex's carefully constructed comfort zone of routine classes, predictable social circles, and quiet evenings spent reading.
